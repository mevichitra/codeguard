# What you will see

- **Squiggles** coloured by severity: critical and high are errors, medium are warnings,
  low and info are hints.
- **Hovers** that explain the risk, link to the CWE, and show the **data flow** from
  untrusted input to the dangerous call.
- The **CodeGuard** activity-bar view, grouping every finding in the workspace by
  severity, category, or file.
- A **status-bar** summary. Click it to jump to the findings.

Performance and quality findings are shown too. Turn categories off with the
`codeguard.categories` setting.
