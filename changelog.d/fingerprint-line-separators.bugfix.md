Source lines are now split on LF, CRLF and CR only, matching how the parsers number them. Before, a form feed (or another Unicode line break such as `\x0b`, `\x85` or U+2028) shifted every later line. Findings after it got fingerprints taken from the wrong line, inline `codeguard: ignore[...]` comments after it suppressed nothing, and editor ranges were misplaced. Fingerprints change only in files that contain such characters. If you have a baseline, run `codeguard baseline update` and then `codeguard baseline prune` once to re-match those findings.
